The first public beta of ryxnroam: pick a place on a live map and apply it to an iPhone, iPad, iOS Simulator or Android Emulator you own or are authorized to test. Teleport, or move along a route at walking, cycling, driving or custom speeds, and stop to restore the real location.

## First launch

These builds are **not signed** with an Apple Developer ID or a Windows code-signing certificate, and they are **not notarized** by Apple, so your computer will warn you the first time.

- **macOS:** open the DMG and drag ryxnroam to Applications. If macOS says it can't verify the app, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to ryxnroam.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click **More info › Run anyway**.

## What you need

- **iPhone / iPad:** `python3 -m pip install -U pymobiledevice3`, Developer Mode on (iOS 16+), and the device trusting your computer. After the first cable connection you can turn on **Use over Wi-Fi** in the app. On Windows, also install Apple Devices (or iTunes).
- **iOS Simulator (Mac only):** Xcode 14 or later.
- **Android Emulator:** Android Studio or the SDK platform-tools (`adb`).

## Beta notes

- Windows and Intel Mac builds have had less testing than Apple silicon so far. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/ryancpjay-ux/ryxnroam/issues) if something breaks.
- Use ryxnroam only on devices you own or are authorized to test. It doesn't bypass device security, managed-device restrictions, parental controls or location-sharing safeguards, and it never runs a simulation without showing it.
